ISL Weekly Archive

Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026. ISL Weekly Archive is a personal local desktop utility maintained by JanielRosario.

Data used

The utility reads Drive file names, identifiers, sizes, parent folders and checksums to recognize existing archive files and verify uploads. It downloads training content the owner is authorized to access and uploads that content to the owner's chosen Google Drive archive. It updates an HTML index in that archive.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials, refresh tokens, source-site sessions, file metadata and execution logs are stored locally on the owner's computer. Archived content is stored in the selected Google Drive. Verified temporary downloads are removed locally after successful upload verification; archived Drive videos are retained. No credentials or training files are published on this website. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to an analytics service.

Google processes authorization requests and Drive transfers; the source platform processes source access. This public informational site is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose own privacy policy governs hosting requests. It has no custom analytics or sign-in form.

Control and retention

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections, disable the local schedule, and remove local credentials, sessions or logs. Revoking access or uninstalling the utility does not delete archived Drive files; those remain under the owner's control.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens, private file links or training content there.
