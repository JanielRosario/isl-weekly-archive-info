ISL Weekly Archive

A personal desktop utility maintained by JanielRosario to archive authorized Insurance Sales Lab training videos in the account owner's Google Drive.

The utility checks for new source videos, preserves previously archived files, organizes uploads by course, and verifies uploads before removing its temporary local copies. It runs on the owner's computer. This informational website does not contain the training archive or offer public sign-up.

Google Drive authorization allows the local utility to inspect its existing archive, create folders and files, and update its HTML index. The requested Drive permission is broader than one folder; the utility's configured operations target the owner's selected archive.

This is an independent personal project, not an official Insurance Sales Lab or Google product.
