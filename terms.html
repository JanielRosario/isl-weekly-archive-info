ISL Weekly Archive

Use of the utility

ISL Weekly Archive is maintained for the account owner's personal archival workflow. It is not a public storage service and is not affiliated with Insurance Sales Lab or Google.

Use requires authorized access to the source material and destination account. The utility does not grant content rights, redistribute training publicly, or bypass source access restrictions. The account owner controls the schedule, destination, permissions and retention of the archived files.

Operation depends on valid sign-ins, source availability, storage capacity and the computer being available. Review failure reports and verify important backups. This page does not modify the source platform's or Google's terms.
